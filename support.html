Getting started

  1. Put your contacts on one tab with headers in row 1 (for example Full Name, Email, Phone).
  2. Open Extensions → Contact Prep → Open Contact Prep. The sidebar guesses your name, phone and email columns; change any that are wrong, or set them to "(skip)".
  3. Choose a phone format and the country to assume for numbers without a +.
  4. Choose where results go (new columns to the right, or a new tab) and click Clean my contacts.

FAQ

Does it change my data? No. It only adds new columns to the right of your data (or creates a new tab).

Does my data leave my spreadsheet? No. The add-on makes no network requests. See Privacy.

Which phone numbers are supported? US and Canada numbers fully (all formats, extensions, 1-800 vanity letters). Numbers starting with + or 00 are converted to +E.164 for any country. Default-country handling for numbers without a + is available for the UK, Australia, New Zealand and Ireland.

Why is a mixed-case name like "DeShawn" left alone? Capitalization is only changed for names typed in ALL CAPS or all lowercase, so deliberate spellings are kept.

How big can my list be? Free: 250 rows per run. Pro: 50,000 rows per run.
