Contact Prep

Split names, fix capitalization, format phone numbers and clean emails in Google Sheets™, before a mail merge or CRM import.

Contact Prep is an add-on for spreadsheets. Pick the name, phone and email columns, click Clean my contacts, and it adds tidy columns next to your data. Existing cells are never changed.

Every result row gets a "check" column that explains anything unusual (invalid phone, possible company name, two people in one cell), so you can review before importing.

Why people use it

  • Mail merge: "Dear [First name]" needs a clean first-name column.
  • CRM and email-tool imports: most importers expect separate first/last name fields and phone numbers in a consistent format (E.164, like +15125550134, is the international standard).
  • Formulas get fiddly: SPLIT and REGEX formulas break on middle names, suffixes, "Last, First" order and surnames like "de la Cruz".

Private by design

Contact Prep runs entirely inside your spreadsheet using Google's Apps Script. It asks only for access to the spreadsheet you open it in, makes no network requests, and sends no data to us or anyone else. See the privacy policy.

How to use

  1. Available as a Google Sheets add-on after Workspace Marketplace approval.
  2. Open your contact list (headers in row 1), then Extensions → Contact Prep → Open Contact Prep.
  3. Check the detected columns, choose a phone format, and click Clean my contacts.
