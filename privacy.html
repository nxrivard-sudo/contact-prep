Privacy Policy

Effective 2026-10-04 · Contact Prep is operated by The Picnic Shoppe LLC (dba The Picnic Collective) · nxrivard@gmail.com

Summary: your spreadsheet data never leaves your spreadsheet.

1. What the add-on accesses. Contact Prep requests two Google permissions: (a) view and manage only the spreadsheet in which you run it (spreadsheets.currentonly), and (b) display its sidebar and dialogs inside Google applications (script.container.ui). It reads the cells of the tab you choose and writes new columns or a new tab in that same spreadsheet.

2. What we collect. Nothing from your spreadsheet. The add-on makes no network requests and does not send names, emails, phone numbers or any other cell contents to us or to any third party. Processing happens inside Google's Apps Script environment, under Google's own terms and privacy policy.

3. License keys. If you buy Pro, your license key is stored in your own Google account's add-on user properties so Pro stays active. It is checked offline and is not sent to us.

4. Purchases. When you buy Pro, the payment processor (Stripe) collects your payment details and the email you provide; we receive your email, the plan and the amount so we can send your key and handle refunds. We never see your full card number.

5. Website. This website uses no cookies and no analytics scripts. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may keep standard server logs (see GitHub's privacy statement).

6. Google user data. Contact Prep's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We do not use Google user data for advertising, do not sell it, and do not transfer it to anyone.

7. Contact and deletion. To remove the add-on, uninstall it from Extensions → Add-ons → Manage add-ons. Purchase records can be deleted on request (subject to tax record-keeping law): nxrivard@gmail.com.
